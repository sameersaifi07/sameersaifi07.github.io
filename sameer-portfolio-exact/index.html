FAQ

GOT QUESTIONS.

What kind of Shopify work do you do?

Everything from new store setups to fully custom builds: theme customisation, custom sections, product page redesigns and conversion fixes. Whether you're a new dropshipper or an established brand, I'll build a store that looks premium and sells.

What do I need to get started?

Just your Shopify login (collaborator access), logo, product details and a few stores you love. Don't have everything ready? No problem. We'll figure it out together on a quick 15-minute call.

Can you redesign my existing Shopify store?

Yes. I can refresh your current theme or rebuild it completely, without losing your products, orders or customer data.

How many revisions do I get?

Starter includes 1 round, Growth includes 2, and Premium continues with 30 days of post-launch support. I share progress at every stage, so there are no surprises at the end.

How long does it take to build my store?

Starter stores are ready in 7 days. Growth and Premium projects usually take 2–4 weeks, depending on pages and custom features. You'll get a clear timeline before we begin.

Who owns the store after it's built?

You do, 100%. The store, design, content and custom sections are fully yours. I build inside your Shopify account, and once the final payment is made, everything is handed over completely.
